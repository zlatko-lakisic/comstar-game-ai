The rules it plays by

Enforced in code, not by good intentions

RuleWhat it means
Fog of warIt sees what a player sees. Every belief carries an age and a confidence that decays.
No cheats, everadd_money, auto_win, force_battle_victory and the rest sit in a hard-blocked set. A run that touches them is tainted and marked.
Declares intentEvery action is declared before it executes. No approval gate, nothing waits — but the record exists.
Single player onlyAutomating multiplayer is cheating other people, which is a different thing entirely.
One advantage it does take, on purpose: it freezes and micros every unit on every tick, faster than any human. That's a deliberate mechanical choice, not an oversight — and the tick rate stays a configurable dial.
